# LangChain Mastery Course

## When to use LangChain

LangChain is one of, if not the, most popular python frameworks.

### Do we even need a framework?

Incredibly simply to use the API of an LLM. However, the minute we begin having more complex use cases, like RAG, agents, or research assistants, frameworks like LangChain come into play.

You can come in not really knowing what a concept like RAG is, and get the benefits without necessarily having a full understanding of that (not entirely recommended).
